Enhancement 1

Software Design and Engineering

I separated the app into model, service, and database layers so that Activities could focus on the user interface. The refactor reduced coupling and gave each part of the application a clearer responsibility.

Algorithms and Data Structures

I added an AnalyticsService with recent weight averages, trends, logging streaks, recent weight change, goal estimates, and plateau detection. A Deque and HashSet helped support calculations that account for missing entries.

Databases

I improved password protection with Argon2id while preserving compatibility with existing PBKDF2 hashes. I also migrated dates to ISO format, reviewed indexes, and replaced delete-and-insert operations with updates where possible.
